# Mapping

o at end that is pronounced /o/ or /ou/ is adapted with o.
Ex: no, so, go, zero, hello, hero, photo, tomato, piano.

o at end that is pronounced /u:/ is adapted with u.
Ex: to, do, who.

ue at end that is pronounced /u:/ is adapted with u.
Ex: blue, true, clue.

ee at end that is pronounced /i:/ is adapted with i.
Ex: three, free, see, tree, agree.

ou at end that is pronounced /u:/ is adapted with u.
Ex: you.

e at end before consonant that is pronounced /i:/ is adapted with i.
Ex: he.
